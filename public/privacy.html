Roguecraft - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Roguecraft ("the Game") is a free browser game made by Krool ("we", "us"). There are no accounts, no ads and no purchases. This page lists what the Game stores, sends and loads.

Google Analytics

The Game loads Google Analytics (Google's gtag.js script from googletagmanager.com) as soon as the page opens. There is no consent prompt and no in-game switch to turn it off. To stop it, block that script with your browser's tracking protection or a content blocker; the Game plays the same without it.

  • Gameplay events we send - run started (ship chosen), run ended (score, wave reached, play time in seconds), ship unlocked, weapon evolved, and boss defeated (boss type and wave). No names or typed text are included.
  • Standard analytics data - Google Analytics records page views and session information such as device and browser type and an approximate location derived from your IP address. It sets its own cookies in your browser to tell sessions apart.

Saved on your device

The Game saves your progress in your browser's local storage. It is not uploaded to us.

  • roguecraft_gamestate - buildings, unlocked and selected ships, unlocked levels, totals (runs, kills), best score and time, per-ship stats and per-level high scores.
  • roguecraft_progression - ship unlocks and purchases.
  • acecraft_run_statistics - per-ship, weapon, passive and evolution statistics, your recent runs, and run and win totals.
  • roguecraft_master_volume, roguecraft_sfx_volume, roguecraft_music_volume, roguecraft_muted - sound settings.

On the GitHub Pages version a service worker also keeps a copy of the Game's files in the browser cache so it loads faster.

Co-op play

Co-op connects two browsers directly to each other (WebRTC, through the PeerJS library). We run no game server.

  • When the main menu opens, the Game connects to the public PeerJS cloud server and registers a random six-digit party code, so a friend can join you. That server sees your IP address and the code.
  • To set up a direct connection, your browser contacts public STUN servers run by Google, Mozilla and stunprotocol.org. They see your IP address.
  • When two players connect, each player's browser learns the other's IP address. Your three-letter party name, chosen ship and gameplay state are sent to the other player and to nobody else.

Hosting

The Game is served by GitHub Pages and by itch.io. Like any web host, they receive your IP address and browser details when you load the page. Their policies: GitHub, itch.io. Google's: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we do not collect

  • No real name, email address, phone number or account credentials. There are no accounts.
  • No precise location.
  • No ads and no advertising SDKs. We do not sell data.

Deleting your data

"Reset Save" on the main menu clears your progress. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything listed under "Saved on your device", including sound settings and run statistics.

Children

The Game does not ask for personal information and none is needed to play.
